# Not too bad

The light at the top says whether weft's service is there. **Green**: it answers. **Amber**: it answers, but some features are off. **Red**: it doesn't, and nothing you send will arrive until it's back.

When a weft has news waiting, the light becomes a bell. Tap it to read what's waiting.

So for now? Not too bad, [all things considered](tip:chat/things).
