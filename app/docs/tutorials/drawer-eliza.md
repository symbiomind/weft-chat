# ELIZA

Joseph Weizenbaum wrote her at MIT in 1966, and she turned sixty in 2026. She was the first program people mistook for a mind.

No engine answers her, and no AI: she is a script, and she knows her way around Weft. Ask her how something works, or tell her your problem.
