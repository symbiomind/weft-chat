# Push to talk

Add a speech-to-text provider here, such as a whisper server, and choose it below. The send shelf then grows a 🎤: hold it and talk.

Every weft hears you with the one chosen here. To try another server, give one weft ears of its own under Hearing on its page, or turn push to talk off there.

What you said lands in the text box first, to read and fix before it goes, unless you ask for it to send as soon as you let go.
