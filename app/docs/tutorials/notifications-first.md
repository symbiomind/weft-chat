# Letters from your wefts

An agent connected through a weft's door can leave you a note: a job finished, a question, a picture. They wait here, every weft's together, newest first.

The light at the top becomes a **bell** while anything waits, and a weft with news wears a **ring** around its colour. The count is in the menu.

Tap a weft's name on a note to talk to it. **Dismiss** is gone for good, on every device.
